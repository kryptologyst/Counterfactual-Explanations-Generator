## Limitations and Risks

### Explanation Instability
- Counterfactual explanations may be unstable or misleading
- Results can vary significantly with different random seeds or parameters
- Explanations may not reflect true causal relationships
- Small changes in input can lead to dramatically different explanations

### Model Dependencies
- Explanations are dependent on the underlying model's behavior
- Biased models will produce biased explanations
- Model errors will be reflected in explanations
- Explanations do not guarantee model correctness

### Ethical Considerations
- Counterfactuals may suggest discriminatory or unfair changes
- Explanations should not be used to justify biased decisions
- Human oversight is essential for all applications
- Consider the impact on affected individuals and groups
